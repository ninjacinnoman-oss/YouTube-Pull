Ninjacinnoman Dashboard

A private analytics dashboard for the Ninjacinnoman YouTube channel. It is used only by the channel's owner.

What it does

Once a day, a small script on the channel owner's computer signs in with Google and reads the channel's own YouTube Analytics and YouTube Data API information:

  • views, engaged views, watch time, subscribers gained and lost, and estimated revenue, per day and per video;
  • the channel's list of videos with their titles, publish dates and privacy status;
  • the public subscriber count;
  • public search results for recent videos about Call of Duty and gaming retrospectives, to show what is trending in the channel's niche.

The script turns this into charts and tables in a private dashboard that only the channel owner can open. It never uploads, edits, deletes or comments on anything.

Access it asks for

Read-only access only: view YouTube Analytics reports, view YouTube Analytics monetary reports, and view the YouTube account.
